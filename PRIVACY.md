# Privacy policy for CarriageHL7

Effective 2 November 2026.

CarriageHL7 is a desktop application published by Carriage. This policy describes what the application does with information on your computer.

## Information the application does not collect

CarriageHL7 does not create an account, and it does not send information to Carriage. It has no analytics, advertising, or crash-reporting service.

HL7 messages, addresses, ports, reply codes, and saved routes stay on your computer unless you send or receive them yourself.

## Information you send

When you send a message, CarriageHL7 opens a connection to the address and port you entered and transmits that message. When Listen is open, it accepts connections on the address and port you entered and can reply to the sender. Those transfers are between your computer and the systems you choose. Carriage does not receive a copy.

## Information stored on your computer

CarriageHL7 stores its settings on your computer. If you turn Log on, it appends send activity to a log file on your computer. You can delete that file. Uninstalling the application does not by itself delete settings or logs that Windows leaves behind.

## Microsoft Store

If you install CarriageHL7 from the Microsoft Store, Microsoft processes the purchase, the trial, and the installation. That activity is covered by Microsoft's privacy statement, not by this policy.
